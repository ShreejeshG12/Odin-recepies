Momos 2

Description: Would you like to have delicious momos at home?

Momos are Tibetan/Nepalese dumplings made by stuffing a simple all-purpose flour dough with spiced vegetables or minced meat, then steaming them until tender. Key techniques include making a thin dough, squeezing moisture from veggies, and folding to seal. They are usually served hot with spicy red chutney.


Ingredients

  • Olive oil
  • Minced meat
  • Wheat Flour
  • Tomatoes

Steps:

  1. Mince meat with spices
  2. Put minced meat into wheat flour
  3. Put tomatoes in a pan and make Achar
  4. Enjoy the delicious momos
